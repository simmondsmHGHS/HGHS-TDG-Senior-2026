The Impacts of
Social Media on
Teens

Social media can often be a large part of teens' lives. It gives them the opportunity to communicate with peers, share posts, build friendships, and build communities.

Social media is also popular among teens because of the increase of dopamine and them being at risk of FOMO because of new trends, events that could be happening in other people's life, and being disconnected from others. It can also give people the opportunity to become hurtful to others as many find it easier as they do not suffer consequences.

Apps like Instagram, Tiktok, Snapchat are some of the most used social media apps by teens. It has become a part of normal routine to check in and sometimes even doom scrolling for hours on these apps.

Positive Impacts

When talking about the impacts of social media it is often when the negative impacts are talked about. And sometimes only the negative impacts. But social media can be a really positive place at times. But how can something that is so well known to be negative be positive?

Negative Impacts

As we all know, social media has its drawbacks and some have even experienced its negativity. Social media can lead to very serious problems. Its use can become addictive, especially in the years 2020 - 2022 when socializing was at an all time low. Is social media really as bad as parents say they are?

Minimise Negative Impacts

It is important to know how to use social media correctly to keep us safe on online platforms. Overall wellbeing is so important for you and loved ones which is why you should be careful with what you do online. With what you share, what you interact with, and more.
