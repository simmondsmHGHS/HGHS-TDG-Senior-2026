Character Design - Xiang Yu

  • Element 1

    When coming up with Xiang Yu's design, there were three aspects I wanted to consider. Her occupation, her personality and the modern xianxia setting. These are important aspects to depict for me, as the more intentional design the more intruiging it is. It also adds subtle storytelling, another aspect you can trust your audiences with so they can come to learn more about your character.

  • Element 2

    Silhouettes are another important factor of character design. I decided to use the shape of her hair to make her face even more 'Cat like' with its fluffiness. For her overall figure in the splash art, I really utilised 'White space', this makes aspects like her ponytail, tail, ears and weapon more clear even without finer details.

  • Elements 3

    Considering the previous element. This technique subtly helps audiences recognise a character more immediately. Oftentimes, this means exaggerating features like the hair like I did. White space can also be used more intentionally, for example the paw shaped rip in Xiang Yu's flag

  • Element 4

    As for her outfit, I brainstormed with many ideas. I first wanted to show more of her 'bold' personality with a more street wear inspired look with talisman to represent the modern xianxia aspects. Then I considered her occupation as a fish mongerer, as it is applicable to the story as well. Then I wanted to try going for a more ancient Chinese style for the xianxia factor.

  • Element 5

    I decided for Xiang Yu to be a calico cat not only as a unique trait for my character but also to subtly represent her personality. Calicos are known for being more assertive and spunky whilst being a little intelligent. Her bright yellow eyes are also representative of her personality, as the trait is associated with hunters.

Character Design - Ru Yingwei

  • Element 1

    Aspects I had to consider for Ru Yingwei's character was mainly her mad passion for research and her high societal upbringing. I also wanted her to feel one-of-a-kind among her peers to further show her status. And of course, I had to somehow incorporate the modern-xianxia genre. Immediately, I decided Ru Yingwei would have albinism. Not only does the pristine white give off a noble aura, but red eyes fit the lunar new year theme. Albinism is also a recessive genetic pattern, that makes her more unique from her peers.

  • Element 2

    In Yingwei's outfit developments, I wanted to lean more into a mad scientist archetype. It is okay, even encouraged for character designers to emphasise a certain archetype as It helps audiences immediately indicate their personality and such.At first, it may seem restricting however with enough creativity you could expand on archetypes: like making a design where a character does not match the personality of their appearance.

  • Element 3

    To merge the mad scientist archetype (which is often depicted in a western setting) I decided to make her 'lab coat' a Qi Pao. This came to me in my own research when I discovered Victorian lab coats resemble the layout of traditional Chinese garments. I also did some research into the Taopunk aesthetic through games like in 'Gui Long Chao' and took inspiration there.

  • Element 4

    I also considered the silhouette of Ru Yingwei to make sure her figure is recognisable This was why I decided to give her a singular tiny braid, resembling a common hairstyle called a 'rat tail'. Not only does it mak her more recognisable, it also makes my design seem more intentional.
