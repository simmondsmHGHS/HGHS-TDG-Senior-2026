History

How was AI made?

AI was developed gradually over many decades— it wasn’t invented in one moment. It grew from early ideas about whether machines could “think” like humans, then became a real scientific field as computers improved.

How AI was made:

AI was created by combining several technologies:

Mathematics – to build logic and calculations
Computer science – to program machines
Data – so AI could learn patterns
Algorithms – step-by-step instructions that help AI make decisions.

Early AI systems were simple and followed fixed rules. For example, some could play board games or solve logic puzzles. Later, scientists developed machine learning, which allowed computers to learn from examples instead of only following pre-written instructions.
