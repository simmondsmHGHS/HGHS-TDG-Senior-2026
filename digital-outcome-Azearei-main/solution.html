Solution

Why using AI matters?
Using AI properly is important because relying on it too much can weaken our critical thinking and problem-solving skills.

If people depend on AI to answer every question or complete every task, they may stop analysing information for themselves. Critical thinking helps us evaluate ideas, make good decisions, and understand what is true or false. AI should be used as a tool to support learning and productivity, not as a replacement for human thinking and creativity.

How to use AI properly?
We can use AI properly by treating it as a tool rather than relying on it to do all the thinking for us.

For example: AI can help generate ideas, explain difficult concepts, or provide information.

But we should still analyse the information, check its accuracy, and form our own conclusions. Asking questions, comparing sources, and solving problems independently before using AI can help maintain and strengthen critical thinking skills. By balancing AI assistance with our own effort, we can benefit from the technology while continuing to develop our creativity, reasoning, and decision-making abilities.
