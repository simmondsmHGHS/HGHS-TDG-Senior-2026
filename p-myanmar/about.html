About US

This site was developed to raise the awareness of the ongoing civil war in Myanmar(Burma). Myanmar recently experienced a severe humanitarian crisis and high civilian casulties. Myanmar has less geographical influence on the global stage. global Peace Index 2025 reveals that conflict in less economically developed or geopolitically prominenet countries gain less news articles per civil. Civil war in Myanmar gain less global awareness than other countries. Between the conflicts in Myanmar, the future of Burmese youths are uncertain. This website raise awareness of the youth voice and their stories.
