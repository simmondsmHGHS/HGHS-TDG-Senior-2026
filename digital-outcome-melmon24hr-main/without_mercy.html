What is 'Without Mercy'?

3ARD Design Project

“Without Mercy” (无慈悲) is a visual novel RPG game introducing a modern reinterpretation of the folklore of the Great Lunar Race through Kemonomimi (humans with animal ears). In a world where a new order of zodiacs will be selected to replace the original, The protagonist (Xiang Yu 香鱼) is the representative of the cat. She intends on revenge on the rat (Ru Yingwei 茹影尾) for the betrayal on her ancestors. It's the player's decision whether or not this revenge plays out smoothly, or an unlikely relationship between the two rivals bloom.

Story Summary

ARC 1

After an accident at her hometown's lunar new year festival, Xiang Yu discovers her cultivation ability and how it relates to her nightmares of rats and betrayal. As a descendant of the cat of the great lunar race, she trains under her master in preparation of an event where she can avenge her ancestors.
