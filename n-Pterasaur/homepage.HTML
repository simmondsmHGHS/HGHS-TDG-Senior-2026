  • Can you imagine seeing a creature as tall as a giraffe fly right over your head? That's what it would have been like to meet a Quetzalcoatlus! Unlike the terrifying dinosaurs in movies, this giant reptile probably wasn't a fierce hunter. Instead, it was a brilliant glider, using warm air currents to stay aloft with hardly any effort.

Is the Quezlacoatlus a dinosaur?

Suprisingly no!

The Quezlacoatlus is an azhdarchid pterosaur which is a seperate lineage from true dinosaurs.
