The Positive Impacts of Social Media on Teens

Contents:

Engage With interests

Creative Outlet

Connection With Others

Social media is not known for its positive impacts but it can have a positive impact on teens as it can be used as a space to engage with others, somewhere to express themselves, and a place to make new friends.

Engage With interests

Depending on interests an social media app will try and formulate a 'for you' page that displays these interests. This gives teens the place to engage with people who are interested in the same things as them.

Creative Outlet

Teens can post a variety of things that matter to them. It gives them the opportunity to post their opinions and creativity.

Connection With Others

Some may find it easier to make friends online as you often find yourself engaging with content that has the same interests as you.
