Myanmar

What is happening

The main cause of the current civil war in Myanmar was the military coup. The civil war started at the start of the 2021. On February 1, 2021, there was a burmese military coup d’état. In the 2020 election, the NLD won the most seats in legislative chambers. The number of military seats decreases. The Myanmar military wants to keep their dominance and power alive in the country. They want to carry on enriching themselves. The military(tamataw) seized power just before the newly elected parliament was about to begin its term after the November 2020 election.
