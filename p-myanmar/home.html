What's Happening?

The current civil war in Myanmar intensified after the military coup on 1 February 2021. In the 2020 election, the National League for Democracy won most of the elected seats in parliament.

The military, known as the Tatmadaw, seized power shortly before the newly elected parliament was due to begin its term. This led to nationwide protests, armed resistance and an ongoing humanitarian crisis.
