Privacy & Misinformation

What is privacy (protecting your privacy online):


Protecting your privacy online means being careful about what you share and who can see it. Personal information like your address, school, age, location or phone number should always be kept private to stay safe.

What you can do to protect it:

  1. Keep social media accounts private
  2. Don’t share personal information (school, address, phone number)
  3. Only accept friend requests from people you know
  4. Turn off location sharing on apps
  5. Use strong, unique passwords

What is misinformation:


Misinformation is false or misleading information that spreads online, especially on social media. It can sometimes look real, so it’s important to think carefully before believing or sharing it. It can also spread quickly through social media and can confuse people or cause harm of believed without checking facts.

What you can do:

  1. Compare information from multiple sources
  2. Check the dates to see if it is current
  3. Question anything that seems unusual or too good to be true
  4. Look at the author or source of the information
  5. Check information on trusted websites
