Cyber Safety For Teens

Welcome To Cyber Safety For Teens

Welcome to your guide to staying safe online. This website is here to help you feel confident and in control when using the internet–whether you’re social media, just chatting with friends, or exploring new apps.
It’s made especially for girls aged 13-15, so everything is easy to understand and relevant to your everyday online life. You will find useful tips, advice, and support to help you protect yourself, make smart choices, and deal with any problems that might come up online.
Being online should feel safe and fun to you and remember, not everyone shares the same fun as you, so you should also respect others and their choices. This site is here to help you make sure that you can safely enjoy your life choices online.

Phishing Scams


Learn how to recognise fake messages or links that try to trick you into giving personal information and how to avoid them safely.

Cyberbullying


Understand what cyberbullying looks like, how it can affect you, and what steps you can take to stay safe and get support.

Privacy & Misinformation


Find out how to protect your personal information and how to tell the difference between real and false information online.
