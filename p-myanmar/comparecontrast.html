Teenagers living

In Myanmar

Access to Education

Conflicts, displacements and damaged schools prevent many burmese children from accessing consistent education.

Everyday Safety

Armed conflicts and landmines regions make travelling, learning and playing outdoors dangerous.

Choices for the Future

Interrupted education and displacement in affected area limit young people's study, employment and their future choices.

Teenagers living

In New Zealand

Access to Education

NZ Students have the ability to enrol at school. They can access a safe and supportive learning environment.

Everyday Safety

Most NZ teenagers can travel to school, meet friends and participate in school activities without experiencing war.

Choices for the Future

Many NZ teenagers can plan further study, training, employment and goals for their future.
