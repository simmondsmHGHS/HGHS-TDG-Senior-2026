How You Could Minimise the Negative Impacts of Social Media on Teens

There are also some ways you could help minimise the impacts of social media. Like setting time limits, blocking accounts that you don't like, and talking to others about what you see online.

Limiting Time Online

Set limits on the time you spend on social media. This can be ever easily done through settings of your phone or the app. This can stop you spending too much time online.

Blocking Content

Blocking or clicking is not interested in posts or accounts that you think are negative. This can stop you seeing these types of posts and improve your overall experience online.

Talking to Someone You Trust

Know that you have people that you can talk to if you need it. You can talk to trusted adults in your life if you think that what you are seeing online is impacting your mental health. This could be a parent, caregiver, or teacher. There is also helpful line you can contact:
