What is AI?

Artificial intelligence (AI) is a type of technology that allows computers and machines to perform tasks that usually require human thinking. It can learn information, recognise objects, identify patterns, and make predictions based on data. AI can also be programmed to work independently without constant human control. It is often used to help people solve problems and complete tasks more efficiently.

5 reasons why AI was made:

Artificial intelligence (AI) wasn’t created for just one single reason—it emerged from a mix of curiosity, practical needs, and technological progress. Here are the main motivations behind why AI was made:

1. To understand intelligence itself
Scientists and philosophers have long wondered what intelligence is and how humans think. Early AI researchers in fields like Computer Science and Cognitive Science tried to build machines that could mimic reasoning, learning, and problem-solving as a way to better understand the human mind.

2. To automate tasks
People have always looked for ways to make work easier. AI allows machines to handle repetitive, complex, or large-scale tasks—like sorting data, recognizing speech, or driving cars—more efficiently than humans in many cases.

3. To solve problems humans struggle with
Some problems are too large or complicated for humans to process alone (like analyzing massive datasets or predicting weather patterns). AI helps by finding patterns and making decisions quickly, especially in areas like medicine, finance, and logistics.

4. To improve decision-making
AI systems can analyze data and suggest or make decisions based on patterns humans might miss. This is used in things like recommendations (movies, products), medical diagnoses, and fraud detection.

5. To create new technology and experiences
AI enables things that didn’t exist before—like voice assistants, real-time translation, and advanced robotics. Companies like OpenAI and Google build AI systems to push these possibilities further.
