Phishing Scams

What is Phishing?


Phishing is when scammers try to trick you into giving away personal information by pretending to be someone you trust, such as a company, website, or even a friend. This can happen through emails, messages, or fake websites that are designed to look real.

Common Signs of a Scam


Scam messages often create urgency or pressure, such as telling you to act quickly or something bad will happen. They may include suspicious links, unusual email addresses, or requests for personal information like passwords. Recognising these warning signs helps you avoid scams before they cause harm.

How to Stay Safe

  1. Do not click suspicious links – Only open links from trusted sources to avoid harmful or fake websites.
  2. Check the sender’s email address – Make sure messages are actually from who they claim to be.
  3. Never share passwords or personal details – Keep your private information secure at all times.
  4. Think before you respond – Scammers often pressure you to act quickly, so take your time.
  5. Use strong, unique passwords – Create passwords that are difficult to guess and different for each account.
